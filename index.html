What file types can I upload?

Audio (MP3, M4A, WAV), text notes, PDFs and slide decks. You can also record directly in the app.

How accurate are the summaries?

Lecta keeps every point linked to the original moment in your lecture, so you can verify anything in one click.

Is my data private?

Your recordings and notes belong to you. We never sell your data or use it to train public models.

Can I use it for any subject?

Yes. From organic chemistry to constitutional law, Lecta adapts its structure to the subject.
